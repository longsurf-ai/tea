Order and fill types, commission and slippage settings, and a deterministic broker emulator that fills strategy orders against bar prices.

A broker turns strategy commands into orders, decides whether, when and at
what price each order fills, charges commission, applies slippage, and
reports each step as an event. The portfolio library applies fills to cash
and positions, and a trade coordinator sequences broker and portfolio on
every bar. A strategy creates a broker with [`broker.new`](./broker/new.md) or
[`broker.basic`](./broker/basic.md) and passes it, with a portfolio, to a coordinator such
as [`trade.nextOpen`](./trade/nextOpen.md), whose page shows a complete strategy; it then
trades through the coordinator rather than calling the broker.
[`broker.BrokerEmulator`](./broker/BrokerEmulator.md) states the execution rules: when orders fill,
at what price and size, and why they are rejected. The
[strategy model](../../strategy.md) describes the responsibility boundary.

The library keeps no global state: each broker value holds its own orders,
ids and matching state. Broker events are appended to one output per event
type, such as `broker.FillExecuted`; each output keeps execution order, but
separate outputs share no ordering.

Add `import broker` to a script to use these as `broker.name`.
