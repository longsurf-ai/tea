Broker contract of [`trade.LotTrade`](../trade/LotTrade.md): executes commands at once instead of scheduling them.

```tea
interface ImmediateBroker
```

It is deliberately narrow: a pure stop check, explicit rejection, and
execution at a price the broker controls. Strategies never choose the fill
price: the coordinator passes the bar's close, and the broker picks between
the stop and a gap open itself.
