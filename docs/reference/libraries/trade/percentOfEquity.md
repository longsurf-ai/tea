Sizes an entry as a percentage of the portfolio's equity, read when `entry` is called.

```tea
trade.percentOfEquity(
    float percent,
    bool commissionIncluded = false
)
```

| Parameter            | Type    | Default | Description                                        |
| -------------------- | ------- | ------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| `percent`            | `float` |         | Percentage of equity, above `0` and at most `100`. |
| `commissionIncluded` | `bool`  | `false` | Whether the budget also pays the commission.       |

**Returns:** `trade.Sizing`: sizing for the `sizing` argument of `entry`.

The equity is the one from the latest `mark` or `end_bar`, or the initial
cash before the first. When the order fills, the budget is divided by the
execution price, the matched price after adverse slippage. With
`commissionIncluded`, the commission comes out of the budget; otherwise it is
charged on top.

The broker checks capital when the order fills, unless the portfolio's margin
for the order's side is `0`. The capital is the cash for a buy and the equity
at the matched price for a sell. With the commission on top, a buy needs
`notional * marginLong / 100 + fee <= cash` and a sell
`notional * marginShort / 100 + fee <= equity`; with the commission included,
the budget must be at most the capital. So with a commission above `0` and a
margin of `100`, the default of [`portfolio.new`](../portfolio/new.md),
`percentOfEquity(100)` is rejected whenever the marked equity is at least
the capital, as on a flat account with no fill since the last mark. A
`percent` of `0` or less, above `100` or `na`, or marked equity that is not
positive, makes the broker reject the order with `invalidQuantity` when it
would fill.

In the formula, B is the budget, E the marked equity, q the quantity, x the
execution price, c the commission setting, f the commission on the fill, m
the margin for the order's side and A the capital. The opening fill of a
reversal pays no per-order commission, as the closing fill already did.

**Formula**

$$
\begin{aligned}
B &= \frac{\mathit{percent}}{100} \cdot E \\
q &= \begin{cases}
B / x & \text{commission on top} \\
B / \bigl(x\,(1 + c)\bigr) & \text{included, commission rate } c \\
B / (x + c) & \text{included, } c \text{ per contract} \\
(B - c) / x & \text{included, } c \text{ per order}
\end{cases} \\
\text{fills only if } & m = 0 \text{ or } \begin{cases}
q\,x\,m / 100 + f \le A & \text{commission on top} \\
B \le A & \text{included}
\end{cases}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Example:** Three coordinators send the same buy on bar 0. One tick of slippage turns bar
1's open of 99 into an execution price of 100, and each order pays a
commission of 5. Half of the 1000 equity buys 500 / 100 = 5 units with the
commission on top, or (500 - 5) / 100 = 4.95 with it included. The 100% order
is rejected: 1000 of notional plus 5 of commission is more than the cash.

```tea
import broker
import portfolio
import trade

fees = broker.commissionCashPerOrder(5.0)
slip = broker.slippageTicks(1.0, 1.0)
var onTop = trade.nextOpen(broker.new(commission = fees, slippage = slip),
    portfolio.new(initialCash = 1000.0))
var included = trade.nextOpen(broker.new(commission = fees, slippage = slip),
    portfolio.new(initialCash = 1000.0))
var whole = trade.nextOpen(broker.new(commission = fees, slippage = slip),
    portfolio.new(initialCash = 1000.0))
onTop.begin_bar(open, bar_index)
included.begin_bar(open, bar_index)
whole.begin_bar(open, bar_index)
if bar_index == 0
    onTop.entry("Long", trade.Direction.long,
        sizing = trade.percentOfEquity(50.0))
    included.entry("Long", trade.Direction.long,
        sizing = trade.percentOfEquity(50.0, true))
    whole.entry("Long", trade.Direction.long,
        sizing = trade.percentOfEquity(100.0))
onTop.end_bar(close, false)
included.end_bar(close, false)
whole.end_bar(close, false)
emit "open" open
emit "on top qty" onTop.position_quantity()
emit "on top cash" onTop.cash()
emit "included qty" included.position_quantity()
emit "included cash" included.cash()
emit "100% qty" whole.position_quantity()
```
