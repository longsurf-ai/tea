Copy of a portfolio's metrics at one moment: cash, position, equity, profit and loss, and trade statistics.

```tea
type PortfolioSnapshot
```

| Field                        | Description                                                                                                                     |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `float cash`                 | Cash balance.                                                                                                                   |
| `float positionQuantity`     | Signed position: positive when long, negative when short, `0` when flat.                                                        |
| `float positionAveragePrice` | Average entry price of the open position; `na` when flat.                                                                       |
| `int openTradeCount`         | Open lots for a lot portfolio; for a net portfolio, the entries counted against `pyramiding` (see [`portfolio.new`](./new.md)). |
| `int maxLongStack`           | Most long lots open at the same time so far; always `0` for a net portfolio.                                                    |
| `int maxShortStack`          | Most short lots open at the same time so far; always `0` for a net portfolio.                                                   |
| `float equity`               | Equity at the latest mark: cash plus the position valued at the mark price; `initialCash` before the first mark.                |
| `float realizedPnl`          | Realized profit and loss, net of commission.                                                                                    |
| `float totalFees`            | Total commission paid.                                                                                                          |
| `int fillCount`              | Number of fills applied.                                                                                                        |
| `int roundTripCount`         | Number of closed trades: closed positions for a net portfolio, closed lots for a lot portfolio.                                 |
| `float winRate`              | Fraction of closed trades with a profit above zero, from `0` to `1`; `0` before the first close.                                |
| `float profitFactor`         | Gross profit divided by gross loss; `0` when there is no loss.                                                                  |
| `float maxDrawdown`          | Largest drop from peak equity at any mark so far, as a fraction of the peak.                                                    |
| `float totalReturn`          | Change in equity since the start, as a fraction of `initialCash`.                                                               |

Get it from a coordinator's `snapshot()`. Cash, position, realized profit,
total commission and the trade statistics follow every fill; `equity`,
`maxDrawdown` and `totalReturn` change only when the portfolio is marked,
by the coordinator's `end_bar` at the close or by its `mark`. Ratios are
fractions: `0.25` means 25%.

In the formula, `p` is the price of the latest mark, and the first line
uses the cash and position at that mark. `E_k` and `P_k` are the equity
and the peak equity at mark `k`. `N` is `roundTripCount`, `W` the number
of closed trades whose profit is above zero, `GP` their total profit, and
`GL` the total loss of the other closed trades. A closed trade's profit is
net of its commission. Right after a mark,
`equity - initialCash - realizedPnl` is the unrealized profit of the open
position, net of the entry commission on the quantity still open.

**Formula**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathit{equity} &= \mathit{cash} + \mathit{positionQuantity} \cdot p \\
\mathit{maxDrawdown} &= \max_k \frac{P_k - E_k}{P_k},
\quad P_k = \max(\mathit{initialCash},\ E_1, \dots, E_k) \\
\mathit{totalReturn} &=
\frac{\mathit{equity} - \mathit{initialCash}}{\mathit{initialCash}} \\
\mathit{winRate} &= \begin{cases} W / N & N > 0 \\ 0 & N = 0 \end{cases} \\
\mathit{profitFactor} &= \begin{cases}
\mathit{GP} / \mathit{GL} & \mathit{GL} > 0 \\ 0 & \mathit{GL} = 0
\end{cases}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Example:** Two round trips of 10 units: bought at 100 and sold at 120, then bought at
120 and sold at 115. `profitFactor` stays `0` until the first losing trade
closes at index 5, then is 200 / 50 = 4. `maxDrawdown` is 0.1 from index 3,
where equity fell to 1080 from its peak of 1200.

```tea
import broker
import portfolio
import trade

var strat = trade.nextOpen(broker.new(), portfolio.new(initialCash = 1000.0))
strat.begin_bar(open, bar_index)
if bar_index == 0 or bar_index == 2
    strat.entry("Long", trade.Direction.long, qty = 10.0)
if bar_index == 1 or bar_index == 4
    strat.close("Exit")
strat.end_bar(close, false)
s = strat.snapshot()
emit "close" close
emit "cash" s.cash
emit "position" s.positionQuantity
emit "equity" s.equity
emit "realized" s.realizedPnl
emit "trades" s.roundTripCount
emit "winRate" s.winRate
emit "profitFactor" s.profitFactor
emit "maxDrawdown" s.maxDrawdown
emit "totalReturn" s.totalReturn
```
