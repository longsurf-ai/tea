Keltner Channels: the exponential moving average of `series`, with bands `mult` average ranges above and below it.

```tea
ta.kc(
    float series,
    simple int length,
    simple float mult,
    simple bool useTrueRange = true
)
```

| Parameter      | Type           | Default | Description                                                                                                                                                             |
| -------------- | -------------- | ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `series`       | `float`        |         | Series to average, usually [`close`](../price-and-volume.md#close).                                                                                                     |
| `length`       | `simple int`   |         | Smoothing length in bars for both averages, at least 1. It must be known when the script starts, such as a constant or an input.                                        |
| `mult`         | `simple float` |         | Band distance in average ranges, such as `2`. It must be known when the script starts, such as a constant or an input.                                                  |
| `useTrueRange` | `simple bool`  | `true`  | `true`, the default, measures each bar's range with `ta.tr(false)`; `false` uses `high - low`. It must be known when the script starts, such as a constant or an input. |

**Returns:** `[float, float, float]`: the middle line, the upper band and the lower band.

The middle line is [`ta.ema`](./ema.md) of `series`. The distance is `mult` times
the [`ta.ema`](./ema.md), over the same `length`, of each bar's range: its true
range ([`ta.tr`](./tr.md)), or `high - low` when `useTrueRange` is `false`. This
average range is an exponential average, not [`ta.atr`](./atr.md).

**Formula**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathit{middle}_t &= \mathrm{ema}(\mathit{series}, \mathit{length})_t \\
\mathit{upper}_t &= \mathit{middle}_t
  + \mathit{mult} \cdot \mathrm{ema}(r, \mathit{length})_t \\
\mathit{lower}_t &= \mathit{middle}_t
  - \mathit{mult} \cdot \mathrm{ema}(r, \mathit{length})_t \\
r_t &= \begin{cases}
  \mathrm{tr}(\text{false})_t & \mathit{useTrueRange} \\
  \mathit{high}_t - \mathit{low}_t & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Warm-up and na:** The middle line is `na` until the first `series` value that is not
`na`. The bands are also `na` until the first range that is not `na`. The
range is `na` when the high or low is `na`, and with `useTrueRange` also
when the previous close is `na`, as on the first bar. After that, a bar
where `series` is `na` keeps the middle line, and a bar where the range is
`na` keeps the average range.

**Example:** The middle line starts at the first close and moves halfway to each new
close. The bands start at index 1, the first bar with a previous close: its
true range is `13 - 10 = 3`, so they sit `2 * 3 = 6` from the middle.

```tea
[middle, upper, lower] = ta.kc(close, 3, 2)
emit "high" high
emit "low" low
emit "close" close
emit "middle" middle
emit "upper" upper
emit "lower" lower
```

```csv
time,high,low,close
0,11,9,10
1,13,11,12
2,12,10,11
3,14,12,13
4,14,12,13
5,16,14,15
```

**Output:**

```text

```
