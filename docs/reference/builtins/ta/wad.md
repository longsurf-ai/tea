Williams accumulation/distribution (WAD): the running total of each bar's move from its true low on an up bar, or from its true high on a down bar.

```tea
series float ta.wad
```

The true low is the lower of [`low`](../price-and-volume.md#low) and the previous [`close`](../price-and-volume.md#close); the
true high is the higher of [`high`](../price-and-volume.md#high) and the previous close. A bar whose
close rose adds `close` minus the true low, a bar whose close fell subtracts
the true high minus `close`, and a bar whose close did not change adds `0`.
The total is in price units; it does not use [`volume`](../price-and-volume.md#volume). Like
[`ta.obv`](./obv.md), it is computed once per bar for the whole script, wherever
the script reads it.

**Formula**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{wad}_t &= \sum_{s \in K_t} x_s,
\qquad K_t = \{\, s \le t : x_s \ne \text{na} \,\} \\
x_s &= \begin{cases}
\mathit{close}_s - \min(\mathit{low}_s, \mathit{close}_{s-1})
& \text{if } \mathit{close}_s > \mathit{close}_{s-1} \\
\mathit{close}_s - \max(\mathit{high}_s, \mathit{close}_{s-1})
& \text{if } \mathit{close}_s < \mathit{close}_{s-1} \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Warm-up and na:** Never `na`: it is `0` on the first bar, which has no previous close.
A bar adds `0` when `close` or the previous close is `na`, and when the
`low` of an up bar or the `high` of a down bar is `na`.

**Example:** At index 3 the previous close, 11, is below the low, so the bar adds
`14 - 11`; at index 5 the previous close, 14, is above the high, so the bar
subtracts `14 - 11`. The close is missing at index 6, so indexes 6 and 7
add `0`.

```tea
emit "high" high
emit "low" low
emit "close" close
emit "wad" ta.wad
```

```csv
time,high,low,close
0,11,9,10
1,13,10,12
2,12,9,11
3,15,12,14
4,14,12,14
5,13,10,11
6,13,11,
7,14,11,13
```

**Output:**

```text
index  high  low  close  wad
0      11    9    10     0
1      13    10   12     2
2      12    9    11     1
3      15    12   14     4
4      14    12   14     4
5      13    10   11     1
6      13    11   na     1
7      14    11   13     1
```
