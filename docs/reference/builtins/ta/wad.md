Williams accumulation/distribution: the running total of each bar's move from its true low on an up bar, or from its true high on a down bar.

```tea
series float ta.wad
```

The true low is the lower of [`low`](../price-and-volume.md#low) and the previous [`close`](../price-and-volume.md#close); the
true high is the higher of [`high`](../price-and-volume.md#high) and the previous close. A bar where
`close` did not change adds `0`.
