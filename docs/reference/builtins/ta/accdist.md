Accumulation/distribution (A/D): the running total of each bar's [`volume`](../price-and-volume.md#volume), weighted by where [`close`](../price-and-volume.md#close) sits between [`low`](../price-and-volume.md#low) and [`high`](../price-and-volume.md#high).

```tea
series float ta.accdist
```

Each bar adds its [`ta.iii`](./iii.md): `volume` when the close is at the high,
`-volume` when it is at the low and `0` at the midpoint. A bar where `high`
equals `low`, whose `ta.iii` is `na`, adds `0`. The total is in units of
volume. Like [`ta.obv`](./obv.md), it is computed once per bar for the whole
script, wherever the script reads it.

**Formula**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{accdist}_t &= \sum_{s \in K_t} x_s
\quad (\text{na when } K_t \text{ is empty}) \\
x_s &= \begin{cases}
0 & \text{if } \mathit{high}_s = \mathit{low}_s \\
\dfrac{2\,\mathit{close}_s - \mathit{low}_s - \mathit{high}_s}
{\mathit{high}_s - \mathit{low}_s} \cdot \mathit{volume}_s
& \text{otherwise}
\end{cases} \\
K_t &= \{\, s \le t : x_s \ne \text{na} \,\}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Warm-up and na:** `na` until a bar adds a value. A bar where `high` equals `low` adds
`0`; any other bar adds nothing while `close`, `high`, `low` or `volume` is
`na`, which leaves the total unchanged.

**Example:** Index 0 adds its volume, with the close at the high; index 1 subtracts its
volume, with the close at the low; index 2 adds `0`, with the close at the
midpoint. Index 4, where `high` equals `low`, adds `0`, and index 5, whose
close is missing, adds nothing.

```tea
emit "high" high
emit "low" low
emit "close" close
emit "volume" volume
emit "accdist" ta.accdist
```
