Variance: how widely the last `length` values of `source` spread around their mean, in squared units.

```tea
ta.variance(float source, int length, bool biased = true)
```

| Parameter | Type    | Default | Description                                                                                                                                          |
| --------- | ------- | ------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `source`  | `float` |         | Series to measure, such as [`close`](../price-and-volume.md#close).                                                                                  |
| `length`  | `int`   |         | Number of bars in the window, at least 1.                                                                                                            |
| `biased`  | `bool`  | `true`  | `true` (the default) divides by the number of known values, giving the population variance; `false` divides by one less, giving the sample variance. |

**Returns:** `float`: the variance, in the units of `source` squared.

Values that are `na` are left out, so the mean is the average of the known
values. Known values that are all equal give exactly `0`, even when they are
decimals.

**Formula**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{variance}_t &= \frac{1}{d_t} \sum_{i \in K_t}
\left( \mathit{source}_{t-i} - \mu_t \right)^2,
\qquad \mu_t = \frac{1}{|K_t|} \sum_{i \in K_t} \mathit{source}_{t-i} \\
d_t &= \begin{cases}
|K_t| & \text{if } \mathit{biased} \\
|K_t| - 1 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases} \\
K_t &= \{\, i : 0 \le i < \mathit{length},
\ \mathit{source}_{t-i} \ne \text{na} \,\}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Warm-up and na:** `na` on the first `length - 1` bars, and when every value in the
window is `na`. With `biased` set to `false`, also `na` when only one value
in the window is known.

**Example:** At index 2 the closes 10, 13 and 16 lie 3, 0 and 3 from their mean, so the
squared distances sum to 18: divided by 3 it is 6, and by 2 it is 9. The
windows that hold the missing close at index 4 have two known values, so
they divide by 2 and 1.

```tea
emit "close" close
emit "population" ta.variance(close, 3)
emit "sample" ta.variance(close, 3, biased = false)
```

```csv
time,close

1,13
2,16

```

**Output:**

```text
index  close  population  sample
0      10     na          na
1      13     na          na
2      16     6           9
3      13     2           3
4      na     2.25        4.5
5      15     1           2
6      12     2.25        4.5
7      12     2           3
```
