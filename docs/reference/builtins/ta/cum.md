Cumulative sum: the running total of `source` over every bar so far.

```tea
ta.cum(float source)
```

| Parameter | Type    | Description                                                          |
| --------- | ------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `source`  | `float` | Series to add up, such as [`volume`](../price-and-volume.md#volume). |

**Returns:** `float`: the sum of every known value of `source` so far.

Values that are `na` are left out: the total keeps its value on those bars.

**Formula**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{cum}_t &= \begin{cases}
\sum_{i \in K_t} \mathit{source}_{t-i} & \text{if } K_t \ne \varnothing \\
\text{na} & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases} \\
K_t &= \{\, i \ge 0 : \mathit{source}_{t-i} \ne \text{na} \,\}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Warm-up and na:** `na` until the first bar where `source` is not `na`.

**Example:** The running total of volume. It is `na` until the first volume at index 1,
and the missing volume at index 4 leaves it at 400.

```tea
emit "volume" volume
emit "cum" ta.cum(volume)
```

```csv
time,volume
0,
1,100
2,200
3,100
4,
5,300
6,200
7,100
```

**Output:**

```text
index  volume  cum
0      na      na
1      100     100
2      200     300
3      100     400
4      na      400
5      300     700
6      200     900
7      100     1000
```
