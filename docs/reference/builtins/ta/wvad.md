Williams variable accumulation/distribution (WVAD) of the current bar: the move from [`open`](../price-and-volume.md#open) to [`close`](../price-and-volume.md#close) as a fraction of the bar's range, times [`volume`](../price-and-volume.md#volume).

```tea
series float ta.wvad
```

It is `volume` when the bar opens at its low and closes at its high, and
`-volume` when it opens at its high and closes at its low. It is not a
running total.

**Formula**

$$
\mathrm{wvad}_t = \frac{\mathit{close}_t - \mathit{open}_t}
{\mathit{high}_t - \mathit{low}_t} \cdot \mathit{volume}_t
$$

**Warm-up and na:** No warm-up: it has a value from the first bar. It is `na` on a bar
where `high` equals `low` or where `open`, `close`, `high`, `low` or
`volume` is `na`.

**Example:** Index 0 opens at its low and closes at its high, so the result is its
volume; index 1 does the opposite. The result is `na` at index 4, where
`high` equals `low`.

```tea
emit "open" open
emit "high" high
emit "low" low
emit "close" close
emit "volume" volume
emit "wvad" ta.wvad
```

```csv
time,open,high,low,close,volume
0,8,12,8,12,100
1,12,12,8,8,200
2,9,12,8,11,300
3,11,12,8,9,400
4,10,10,10,10,500
5,11,14,10,13,200
```

**Output:**

```text
index  open  high  low  close  volume  wvad
0      8     12    8    12     100     100
1      12    12    8    8      200     -200
2      9     12    8    11     300     150
3      11    12    8    9      400     -200
4      10    10    10   10     500     na
5      11    14    10   13     200     100
```
