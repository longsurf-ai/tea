On-balance volume (OBV): the running total of [`volume`](../price-and-volume.md#volume), added on bars where [`close`](../price-and-volume.md#close) rose and subtracted on bars where it fell.

```tea
series float ta.obv
```

Each bar adds `math.sign(ta.change(close)) * volume`: its volume when the
close rose from the previous bar, minus its volume when it fell, and `0` when
it did not change. The total is in units of volume.

`ta.obv` is a variable, not a call: it is computed once per bar for the whole
script, so every read sees the same value, even one inside an `if`. The call
`ta.cum(math.sign(ta.change(close)) * volume)` gives the same value only
when it runs on every bar.

**Formula**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{obv}_t &= \sum_{s \in K_t} x_s
\quad (\text{na when } K_t \text{ is empty}) \\
x_s &= \operatorname{sign}(\mathit{close}_s - \mathit{close}_{s-1})
\cdot \mathit{volume}_s \\
K_t &= \{\, s \le t : x_s \ne \text{na} \,\}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Warm-up and na:** `na` on the first bar, which has no previous close, and until the
first bar where `close`, the previous close and `volume` are all known. A
later bar where any of them is `na` leaves the total unchanged, so a missing
close holds it for two bars: its own and the next.

**Example:** OBV is `na` on the first bar and holds at index 3, where the close did not
change. The close is missing at index 5, so OBV holds there and at index 6.

```tea
emit "close" close
emit "volume" volume
emit "obv" ta.obv
```

```csv
time,close,volume
0,10,100
1,11,200
2,10,150
3,10,300
4,12,250
5,,100
6,13,400
7,12,200
```

**Output:**

```text
index  close  volume  obv
0      10     100     na
1      11     200     200
2      10     150     50
3      10     300     50
4      12     250     300
5      na     100     300
6      13     400     300
7      12     200     100
```

**Example:** The `if` reads `ta.obv` only from index 3 on, yet `late` equals `obv` there:
the variable counted the earlier bars too.

```tea
float late = na
if bar_index >= 3
    late := ta.obv
emit "close" close
emit "volume" volume
emit "obv" ta.obv
emit "late" late
```

```csv
time,close,volume
0,10,100
1,11,200
2,10,150
3,12,300
4,13,250
5,11,100
```

**Output:**

```text
index  close  volume  obv  late
0      10     100     na   na
1      11     200     200  na
2      10     150     50   na
3      12     300     350  350
4      13     250     600  600
5      11     100     500  500
```
