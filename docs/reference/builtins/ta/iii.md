Intraday intensity index (III) of the current bar: where [`close`](../price-and-volume.md#close) sits between [`low`](../price-and-volume.md#low) and [`high`](../price-and-volume.md#high), times [`volume`](../price-and-volume.md#volume).

```tea
series float ta.iii
```

It is `volume` when the close is at the high, `-volume` when it is at the
low and `0` at the midpoint. It is not a running total; [`ta.accdist`](./accdist.md)
adds it up.

**Formula**

$$
\mathrm{iii}_t = \frac
{2\,\mathit{close}_t - \mathit{high}_t - \mathit{low}_t}
{\mathit{high}_t - \mathit{low}_t} \cdot \mathit{volume}_t
$$

**Warm-up and na:** No warm-up: it has a value from the first bar. It is `na` on a bar
where `high` equals `low` or where `close`, `high`, `low` or `volume` is
`na`.

**Example:** The result is `volume` at index 0, where the close is at the high, `-volume`
at index 1, where it is at the low, and `0` at index 2, where it is at the
midpoint. It is `na` at index 4, where `high` equals `low`.

```tea
emit "high" high
emit "low" low
emit "close" close
emit "volume" volume
emit "iii" ta.iii
```

```csv
time,high,low,close,volume
0,12,8,12,100
1,12,8,8,200
2,12,8,10,300
3,12,8,11,400
4,10,10,10,500
5,14,10,13,200
```

**Output:**

```text
index  high  low  close  volume  iii
0      12    8    12     100     100
1      12    8    8      200     -200
2      12    8    10     300     0
3      12    8    11     400     200
4      10    10   10     500     na
5      14    10   13     200     100
```
