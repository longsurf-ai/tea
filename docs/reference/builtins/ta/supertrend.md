Supertrend: a trailing line below price in an uptrend and above it in a downtrend, with the trend direction.

```tea
ta.supertrend(float factor, simple int atrPeriod)
```

| Parameter   | Type         | Description                                                                                                              |
| ----------- | ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `factor`    | `float`      | Band distance in average true ranges, such as `3`.                                                                       |
| `atrPeriod` | `simple int` | Length of the [`ta.atr`](./atr.md), at least 1. It must be known when the script starts, such as a constant or an input. |

**Returns:** `[float, int]`: the line, then the direction: `-1` in an uptrend and `1` in a downtrend.

Two bands sit `factor` times [`ta.atr`](./atr.md) above and below [`hl2`](../price-and-volume.md#hl2). The
lower band never falls unless the previous [`close`](../price-and-volume.md#close) was below it, and
the upper band never rises unless the previous close was above it. In a
downtrend the line is the upper band, and the trend turns up when `close`
rises above it; in an uptrend the line is the lower band, and the trend
turns down when `close` falls below it.

**Formula**

$$
\begin{aligned}
A_t &= \mathrm{atr}(\mathit{atrPeriod})_t, \quad
  \hat{U}_t = \mathit{hl2}_t + \mathit{factor} \cdot A_t, \quad
  \hat{L}_t = \mathit{hl2}_t - \mathit{factor} \cdot A_t \\
U_t &= \begin{cases}
  \hat{U}_t & \hat{U}_t < U'_t \text{ or } \mathit{close}_{t-1} > U'_t \\
  U'_t & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases} \qquad U'_t = \mathrm{nz}(U_{t-1}) \\
L_t &= \begin{cases}
  \hat{L}_t & \hat{L}_t > L'_t \text{ or } \mathit{close}_{t-1} < L'_t \\
  L'_t & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases} \qquad L'_t = \mathrm{nz}(L_{t-1}) \\
\mathit{direction}_t &= \begin{cases}
  1 & \text{if } A_{t-1} = \text{na} \\
  -1 \text{ if } \mathit{close}_t > U_t \text{, else } 1
    & \text{else if } \mathit{line}_{t-1} = U'_t \\
  1 \text{ if } \mathit{close}_t < L_t \text{, else } -1 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases} \\
\mathit{line}_t &= \begin{cases}
  L_t & \mathit{direction}_t = -1 \\
  U_t & \mathit{direction}_t = 1
\end{cases}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Warm-up and na:** `direction` is `1` up to and including the bar where the ATR gets
its first value: the first `atrPeriod` bars when no high or low is missing.
With prices above `0`, `line` is `0` on the first bar and `na` on the later
bars where the ATR is `na`; a missing close before the ATR's first value
makes `line` `0` on the next bar. After that, a bar whose `close` is `na`
keeps the direction. On a bar whose [`hl2`](../price-and-volume.md#hl2) is `na`, a band keeps its
previous value, or is `na` if the previous close was beyond it.

**Example:** After the placeholder `0` at index 0, the line is the upper band,
`hl2 + atr`: `10.5 + 2.5 = 13`, then 12.25, where it stays because that
band does not rise. At index 5 the close of 14 is above it, so `direction`
turns to `-1` and the line drops to the lower band, 8.

```tea
[line, direction] = ta.supertrend(1, 2)
emit "high" high
emit "low" low
emit "close" close
emit "atr" ta.atr(2)
emit "line" line
emit "direction" direction
```

