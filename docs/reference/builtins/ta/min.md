All-time low: the lowest value of `source` on any bar so far.

```tea
ta.min(float source)
```

| Parameter | Type    | Description                                                   |
| --------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `source`  | `float` | Series to track, such as [`low`](../price-and-volume.md#low). |

**Returns:** `float`: the lowest known value of `source` so far.

Values that are `na` are left out: the result keeps its value on those bars.
Compare [`ta.lowest`](./lowest.md), which looks back a fixed number of bars.

**Formula**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{min}_t &= \min_{i \in K_t} \mathit{source}_{t-i} \\
K_t &= \{\, i \ge 0 : \mathit{source}_{t-i} \ne \text{na} \,\}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Warm-up and na:** `na` until the first bar where `source` is not `na`.

**Example:** The lowest low so far. It is `na` until the first low at index 1, and the
missing low at index 4 leaves it at 8.

```tea
emit "low" low
emit "min" ta.min(low)
```

```csv
time,low
0,
1,10
2,8
3,9
4,
5,7
6,8
7,6
```

**Output:**

```text
index  low  min
0      na   na
1      10   10
2      8    8
3      9    8
4      na   8
5      7    7
6      8    7
7      6    6
```
