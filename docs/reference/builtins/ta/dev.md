Mean absolute deviation: the average distance of the last `length` values of `source` from their mean.

```tea
ta.dev(float source, int length)
```

| Parameter | Type    | Description                                                         |
| --------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `source`  | `float` | Series to measure, such as [`close`](../price-and-volume.md#close). |
| `length`  | `int`   | Number of bars in the window, at least 1.                           |

**Returns:** `float`: the mean absolute deviation, in the units of `source`.

The mean is the [`ta.sma`](./sma.md) of the same window. Values that are `na` are
left out of both the mean and the average distance.

**Formula**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{dev}_t &= \frac{1}{|K_t|} \sum_{i \in K_t}
\left| \mathit{source}_{t-i} - \mu_t \right|,
\qquad \mu_t = \mathrm{sma}(\mathit{source}, \mathit{length})_t \\
K_t &= \{\, i : 0 \le i < \mathit{length},
\ \mathit{source}_{t-i} \ne \text{na} \,\}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Warm-up and na:** `na` on the first `length - 1` bars, and when every value in the
window is `na`.

**Example:** At index 2 the closes 9, 12 and 15 lie 3, 0 and 3 from their mean of 12, so
the deviation is 2. The windows that hold the missing close at index 4 use
their two known values.

```tea
emit "close" close
emit "sma" ta.sma(close, 3)
emit "dev" ta.dev(close, 3)
```

```csv
time,close
0,9
1,12
2,15
3,9
4,
5,9
6,12
7,15
```

**Output:**

```text
index  close  sma   dev
0      9      na    na
1      12     na    na
2      15     12    2
3      9      12    2
4      na     12    3
5      9      9     0
6      12     10.5  1.5
7      15     12    2
```
