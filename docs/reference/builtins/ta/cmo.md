Chande Momentum Oscillator (CMO) of `series`, from `-100` to `100`.

```tea
ta.cmo(float series, int length)
```

| Parameter | Type    | Description                                                         |
| --------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `series`  | `float` | Series to measure, usually [`close`](../price-and-volume.md#close). |
| `length`  | `int`   | Number of bar-to-bar changes in the window, at least 1.             |

**Returns:** `float`: the oscillator.

Over the last `length` bar-to-bar changes, it is the sum of the gains minus
the sum of the losses, as a percentage of their total. A change that
involves an `na` value is left out.

**Formula**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_t &= \mathit{series}_t - \mathit{series}_{t-1} \\
K_t &= \{\, i : 0 \le i < \mathit{length},\
\Delta_{t-i} \ne \text{na} \,\} \\
G_t &= \sum_{i \in K_t} \max(\Delta_{t-i}, 0), \qquad
L_t = \sum_{i \in K_t} \max(-\Delta_{t-i}, 0) \\
\mathrm{cmo}_t &= 100 \cdot \frac{G_t - L_t}{G_t + L_t}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Warm-up and na:** `na` on the first `length` bars, when no change in the window is
known, and when every known change is `0`.

**Example:** At index 2 a gain of 2 and a loss of 1 give `100 * (2 - 1) / (2 + 1)`. The
missing close at index 6 leaves out the changes into and out of it, so
index 7 has none left and is `na`.

```tea
emit "close" close
emit "cmo" ta.cmo(close, 2)
```
