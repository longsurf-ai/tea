Wilder's smoothed moving average of `source`, the running average behind [`ta.rsi`](./rsi.md), [`ta.atr`](./atr.md) and [`ta.dmi`](./dmi.md).

```tea
ta.rma(float source, simple int length)
```

| Parameter | Type         | Description                                                                                                                                                    |
| --------- | ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `source`  | `float`      | Series to average.                                                                                                                                             |
| `length`  | `simple int` | Smoothing length in bars, at least 1; also the number of values in the starting mean. It must be known when the script starts, such as a constant or an input. |

**Returns:** `float`: the average.

It starts at the mean of the first `length` values of `source` that are not
`na`, then moves toward each new value by `1 / length` of the difference. A
bar where `source` is `na` is skipped: it does not count toward the first
`length` values, and later it keeps the previous result. [`ta.ema`](./ema.md)
moves by `2 / (length + 1)` instead and starts at the first value.

**Formula**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{rma}_t &= \begin{cases}
\text{na} & \text{if } c_t < \mathit{length} \\
\dfrac{1}{\mathit{length}}
\displaystyle\sum_{i \in K_t} \mathit{source}_{t-i}
& \text{if } c_{t-1} < c_t = \mathit{length} \\
\mathrm{rma}_{t-1} & \text{if } \mathit{source}_t = \text{na} \\
\alpha \cdot \mathit{source}_t + (1 - \alpha) \cdot \mathrm{rma}_{t-1}
& \text{otherwise}
\end{cases} \\
\text{where}\quad
K_t &= \{\, i \ge 0 : \mathit{source}_{t-i} \ne \text{na} \,\},
\quad c_t = |K_t|,
\quad \alpha = \frac{1}{\mathit{length}}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Warm-up and na:** `na` until `length` values of `source` that are not `na` have
arrived, on consecutive bars or not. After that it is never `na`: a bar
where `source` is `na` keeps the previous result.

**Example:** A 3-bar average. The close at index 1 is missing, so the third value
arrives at index 3, where the average starts at `(10 + 12 + 14) / 3 = 12`.
Each later close moves it a third of the way, and the missing close at
index 5 keeps 14.

```tea
emit "close" close
emit "rma" ta.rma(close, 3)
```

```csv
time,close
0,10
1,
2,12
3,14
4,18
5,
6,20
7,22
```

**Output:**

```text
index  close  rma
0      10     na
1      na     na
2      12     na
3      14     12
4      18     14
5      na     14
6      20     16
7      22     18
```
