Rate of change (ROC): the percentage change of `source` from its value `length` bars ago.

```tea
ta.roc(float source, int length)
```

| Parameter | Type    | Description                                                         |
| --------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `source`  | `float` | Series to compare, usually [`close`](../price-and-volume.md#close). |
| `length`  | `int`   | Number of bars back, at least 1.                                    |

**Returns:** `float`: the change as a percentage of the earlier value.

**Formula**

$$
\mathrm{roc}_t = 100 \cdot
\frac{\mathit{source}_t - \mathit{source}_{t-\mathit{length}}}
{\mathit{source}_{t-\mathit{length}}}
$$

**Warm-up and na:** `na` on the first `length` bars, when either value is `na`, and
when the earlier value is `0`.

**Example:** The rate of change over 2 bars: from 10 to 25 is 150 at index 2. The close
two bars before index 6 is 0, so that value is `na`.

```tea
emit "close" close
emit "roc" ta.roc(close, 2)
```

```csv
time,close
0,10
1,20
2,25
3,30
4,0
5,15
6,40
7,30
```

**Output:**

```text
index  close  roc
0      10     na
1      20     na
2      25     150
3      30     50
4      0      -100
5      15     -50
6      40     na
7      30     100
```
