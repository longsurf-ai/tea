True range of the current bar: its high-to-low range, extended to reach the previous close.

```tea
ta.tr(simple bool handle_na)
```

| Parameter   | Type          | Description                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `handle_na` | `simple bool` | Result when the previous close is `na`, as on the first bar: `true` gives `high - low`; `false` gives `na`. It must be known when the script starts, such as a constant or an input. |

**Returns:** `float`: the true range.

It measures how far price moved during the bar, counting a gap from the
previous [`close`](../price-and-volume.md#close), which comes from the bar before even when the call
did not run on that bar. When that close lies between this bar's
[`low`](../price-and-volume.md#low) and [`high`](../price-and-volume.md#high), the true range is `high - low`. It is in
price units. [`ta.atr`](./atr.md) averages it. Pine Script's variable `ta.tr` is
the same as `ta.tr(false)`.

**Formula**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{tr}_t &= \begin{cases}
\max(\mathit{high}_t - \mathit{low}_t,\ |\mathit{high}_t - c|,\
|\mathit{low}_t - c|) & c \ne \text{na} \\
\mathit{high}_t - \mathit{low}_t
& c = \text{na} \text{ and } \mathit{handle\_na} \\
\text{na} & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases} \\
\text{where}\quad c &= \mathit{close}_{t-1}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Warm-up and na:** No warm-up when `handle_na` is `true`. When it is `false`, `na` on
the first bar and on the bar after each missing close. A missing high or low
gives `na` either way.

**Example:** At index 2 the bar is above the previous close of 11, so the true range is
`15 - 11 = 4`, more than `high - low`. Index 0, and index 4 after the
missing close, have no previous close: `ta.tr(true)` gives `high - low` and
`ta.tr(false)` gives `na`.

```tea
emit "high" high
emit "low" low
emit "close" close
emit "tr(true)" ta.tr(true)
emit "tr(false)" ta.tr(false)
```

```csv
time,high,low,close
0,11,9,10
1,12,10,11
2,15,13,14
3,14,12,
4,13,11,12
5,10,8,9
```

**Output:**

```text
index  high  low  close  tr(true)  tr(false)
0      11    9    10     2         na
1      12    10   11     2         2
2      15    13   14     4         4
3      14    12   na     2         2
4      13    11   12     2         na
5      10    8    9      4         4
```
