Hull moving average of `source`, built from three [`ta.wma`](./wma.md) averages to reduce lag.

```tea
ta.hma(float source, simple int length)
```

| Parameter | Type         | Description                                                                                                                               |
| --------- | ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `source`  | `float`      | Series to average, usually [`close`](../price-and-volume.md#close).                                                                       |
| `length`  | `simple int` | Number of bars in the longest of the three averages, at least 2. It must be known when the script starts, such as a constant or an input. |

**Returns:** `float`: the average.

It is a [`ta.wma`](./wma.md) over `math.floor(math.sqrt(length))` bars of
`2 * ta.wma(source, length / 2) - ta.wma(source, length)`, with
`length / 2` rounded down. Each of the three averages leaves out `na`
values.

The difference first exists on the bar where `ta.wma(source, length)`
fills, and the result waits until the outer average holds
`math.floor(math.sqrt(length))` differences, so it starts
`math.floor(math.sqrt(length)) - 1` bars after `ta.wma(source, length)`:
at index 8 rather than 7 for a `length` of 8. The formula applies from
then on.

**Formula**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{hma}_t &= \mathrm{wma}(d, r)_t \\
d_t &= 2 \cdot \mathrm{wma}(\mathit{source}, h)_t
- \mathrm{wma}(\mathit{source}, \mathit{length})_t \\
\text{where}\quad h &= \lfloor \mathit{length} / 2 \rfloor,
\quad r = \lfloor \sqrt{\mathit{length}} \rfloor
\end{aligned}
$$

**Warm-up and na:** `na` on the first `length + math.floor(math.sqrt(length)) - 2`
bars: 4 for a `length` of 4, 10 for 9. After that, `na` values are left
out, and the result is `na` only while the last
`math.floor(length / 2) + math.floor(math.sqrt(length)) - 1` values of
`source` are all `na`: 3 for a `length` of 4, 6 for 9.

**Example:** With a `length` of 4 the first value is at index
`4 + math.floor(math.sqrt(4)) - 2 = 4`. While the closes rise by 1 a bar it
equals the close. The close at index 8 is missing; the averages leave it
out, so the result there is still a number.

```tea
emit "close" close
emit "hma" ta.hma(close, 4)
```
