Percent rank: the percentage of the previous `length` values of `source` that are less than or equal to its current value.

```tea
ta.percentrank(float source, int length)
```

| Parameter | Type    | Description                                                      |
| --------- | ------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `source`  | `float` | Series to rank, such as [`close`](../price-and-volume.md#close). |
| `length`  | `int`   | Number of previous values to compare with, at least 1.           |

**Returns:** `float`: a percentage from `0` to `100`, in steps of `100 / length`.

The current value is compared with the `length` values before it, not with
itself. An `na` value, current or previous, is not left out: it makes the
result `na`.

**Formula**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{percentrank}_t &= \begin{cases}
\text{na} & \text{if } \mathit{source}_{t-i} = \text{na}
\text{ for some } 0 \le i \le \mathit{length} \\
\dfrac{100}{\mathit{length}} \cdot |C_t| & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases} \\
\text{where}\quad C_t &= \{\, i : 1 \le i \le \mathit{length},
\ \mathit{source}_{t-i} \le \mathit{source}_t \,\}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Warm-up and na:** `na` on the first `length` bars. An `na` value of `source` makes the
result `na` on its own bar and on the `length` bars after it.

**Example:** At index 5 the close 12 is greater than or equal to 11, 12 and 9 but not 13,
so it ranks 75. The missing close at index 6 makes the results at indexes 6
to 10 `na`, and at index 11 the close ranks again.

```tea
emit "close" close
emit "rank" ta.percentrank(close, 4)
```

```csv
time,close
0,10
1,13
2,11
3,12
4,9
5,12

7,14

9,12
10,13

```

```text
index  close  rank
0      10     na
1      13     na
2      11     na

4      9      0
5      12     75
6      na     na
7      14     na
8      8      na
9      12     na
10     13     na
11     11     25
```
