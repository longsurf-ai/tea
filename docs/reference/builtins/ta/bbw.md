Bollinger Band width: the distance between the upper and lower [`ta.bb`](./bb.md) bands as a percentage of the middle band.

```tea
ta.bbw(float series, int length, simple float mult)
```

| Parameter | Type           | Description                                                                                                                 |
| --------- | -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `series`  | `float`        | Series to measure, usually [`close`](../price-and-volume.md#close).                                                         |
| `length`  | `int`          | Number of bars in the window, at least 1.                                                                                   |
| `mult`    | `simple float` | Band distance in standard deviations, such as `2`. It must be known when the script starts, such as a constant or an input. |

**Returns:** `float`: the width in percent of the middle band.

It is small when the values in the window are close together. Unlike
[`ta.kcw`](./kcw.md), it is multiplied by `100`.

**Formula**

$$
\mathrm{bbw}_t = 100 \cdot \frac{2 \cdot \mathit{mult}
  \cdot \mathrm{stdev}(\mathit{series}, \mathit{length})_t}
  {\mathrm{sma}(\mathit{series}, \mathit{length})_t}
$$

**Warm-up and na:** `na` on the first `length - 1` bars, when every value in the window
is `na`, and when the middle band is `0`. Other `na` values are left out of
the window.

**Example:** While the closes swing between 19 and 21, the middle band is 20 and the
standard deviation 1, so the width is `100 * 2 * 2 * 1 / 20 = 20`. The jump
to 25 at index 6 widens it.

```tea
emit "close" close
emit "bbw" ta.bbw(close, 4, 2)
```

```csv
time,close
0,19
1,21
2,19
3,21
4,19
5,21
6,25
7,25
```
