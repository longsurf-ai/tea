Price-volume trend (PVT): the running total of each bar's [`volume`](../price-and-volume.md#volume), weighted by the relative change of [`close`](../price-and-volume.md#close).

```tea
series float ta.pvt
```

Each bar adds `ta.change(close) / close[1] * volume`: its volume times the
fraction by which the close moved from the previous bar, so a 10% rise adds
a tenth of the volume. The total is in units of volume. Like
[`ta.obv`](./obv.md), it is computed once per bar for the whole script, wherever
the script reads it.

**Formula**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{pvt}_t &= \sum_{s \in K_t} x_s
\quad (\text{na when } K_t \text{ is empty}) \\
x_s &= \frac{\mathit{close}_s - \mathit{close}_{s-1}}{\mathit{close}_{s-1}}
\cdot \mathit{volume}_s \\
K_t &= \{\, s \le t : x_s \ne \text{na} \,\}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Warm-up and na:** `na` on the first bar, which has no previous close, and until the
first bar where `close`, the previous close and `volume` are all known and
the previous close is not `0`. A later bar that fails this leaves the total
unchanged, so a missing close holds it for two bars: its own and the next.

**Example:** PVT is `na` on the first bar; at index 1 the close rises 10% and adds 20,
a tenth of the volume. The close is missing at index 5, so PVT holds there
and at index 6.

```tea
emit "close" close
emit "volume" volume
emit "pvt" ta.pvt
```

```csv
time,close,volume
0,10,100
1,11,200
2,10,110
3,10,300
4,12,250
5,,100
6,15,400
7,12,200
```

**Output:**

```text
index  close  volume  pvt
0      10     100     na
1      11     200     20
2      10     110     10
3      10     300     10
4      12     250     60
5      na     100     60
6      15     400     60
7      12     200     20
```
