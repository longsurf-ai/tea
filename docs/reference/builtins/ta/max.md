All-time high: the highest value of `source` on any bar so far.

```tea
ta.max(float source)
```

| Parameter | Type    | Description                                                     |
| --------- | ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `source`  | `float` | Series to track, such as [`high`](../price-and-volume.md#high). |

**Returns:** `float`: the highest known value of `source` so far.

Values that are `na` are left out: the result keeps its value on those bars.
Compare [`ta.highest`](./highest.md), which looks back a fixed number of bars.

**Formula**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{max}_t &= \max_{i \in K_t} \mathit{source}_{t-i} \\
K_t &= \{\, i \ge 0 : \mathit{source}_{t-i} \ne \text{na} \,\}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Warm-up and na:** `na` until the first bar where `source` is not `na`.

**Example:** The highest high so far. It is `na` until the first high at index 1, and the
missing high at index 4 leaves it at 12.

```tea
emit "high" high
emit "max" ta.max(high)
```

```csv
time,high
0,
1,10
2,12
3,11
4,
5,14
6,13
7,15
```

**Output:**

```text
index  high  max
0      na    na
1      10    10
2      12    12
3      11    12
4      na    12
5      14    14
6      13    14
7      15    15
```
