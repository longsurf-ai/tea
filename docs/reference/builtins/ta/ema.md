Exponential moving average of `source`: a running average that gives recent values more weight.

```tea
ta.ema(float source, simple int length)
```

| Parameter | Type         | Description                                                                                                                                     |
| --------- | ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `source`  | `float`      | Series to average, usually [`close`](../price-and-volume.md#close).                                                                             |
| `length`  | `simple int` | Smoothing length in bars, at least 1; larger values react more slowly. It must be known when the script starts, such as a constant or an input. |

**Returns:** `float`: the average.

Each bar moves the average toward `source` by `2 / (length + 1)` of the
difference, so the weight of older values falls off exponentially. The
average starts at the first value of `source` that is not `na`, without
waiting for `length` bars, and a bar where `source` is `na` leaves it
unchanged. [`ta.rma`](./rma.md) moves by `1 / length` instead and waits for
`length` values.

**Formula**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{ema}_t &= \begin{cases}
\mathrm{ema}_{t-1} & \text{if } \mathit{source}_t = \text{na} \\
\mathit{source}_t & \text{if } \mathrm{ema}_{t-1} = \text{na} \\
\alpha \cdot \mathit{source}_t + (1 - \alpha) \cdot \mathrm{ema}_{t-1}
& \text{otherwise}
\end{cases} \\
\text{where}\quad \alpha &= \frac{2}{\mathit{length} + 1}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Warm-up and na:** `na` until the first bar where `source` is not `na`. After that it
is never `na`: a bar where `source` is `na` keeps the previous result.

**Example:** A 3-bar average moves half of the way, `2 / (3 + 1)`, toward each close.
It is `na` before the first close and starts at that close, 10 at index 1.
The missing close at index 5 keeps the previous value, 13.

```tea
emit "close" close
emit "ema" ta.ema(close, 3)
```

```csv
time,close
0,
1,10
2,12
3,11
4,15
5,
6,17
7,19
```

**Output:**

```text
index  close  ema
0      na     na
1      10     10
2      12     11
3      11     11
4      15     13
5      na     13
6      17     15
7      19     17
```
