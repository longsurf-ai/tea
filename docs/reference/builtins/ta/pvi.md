Positive volume index (PVI): starts at `1` and moves with [`close`](../price-and-volume.md#close), by its relative change, only on bars whose [`volume`](../price-and-volume.md#volume) rose from the previous bar.

```tea
series float ta.pvi
```

On such a bar it changes by the same percentage as the close; on any other
bar it keeps its value. It is a ratio without a unit. [`ta.nvi`](./nvi.md) moves on
the bars where volume fell, and a bar whose volume did not change moves
neither. Like [`ta.obv`](./obv.md), it is computed once per bar for the whole
script, wherever the script reads it.

**Formula**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{pvi}_t &= \begin{cases}
\mathrm{pvi}_{t-1} + \dfrac{\mathit{close}_t - \mathit{close}_{t-1}}
{\mathit{close}_{t-1}} \cdot \mathrm{pvi}_{t-1} & \text{if } c_t \\
\mathrm{pvi}_{t-1} & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases} \\
\text{where}\quad c_t &= \big(\mathit{volume}_t > \operatorname{nz}(\mathit{volume}_{t-1})\big)
\land \big(\mathit{close}_t, \mathit{close}_{t-1} \notin \{0, \text{na}\}\big) \\
\mathrm{pvi} &= 1 \text{ before the first bar}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Warm-up and na:** Never `na`: it is `1` on the first bar. A bar leaves it unchanged
when `close` or the previous close is `na` or `0`, or when `volume` is `na`.
A previous `volume` that is `na` counts as `0`.

**Example:** PVI moves only where volume rose: up 50% with the close at index 1 and up
100% at index 4. Volume is missing at index 5, so PVI holds there; at index 6
the missing volume counts as `0`, so PVI rises 50% with the close.

```tea
emit "close" close
emit "volume" volume
emit "pvi" ta.pvi
```

```csv
time,close,volume
0,8,100
1,12,200
2,9,100
3,9,100
4,18,150
5,12,
6,18,50
7,9,25
```

**Output:**

```text
index  close  volume  pvi
0      8      100     1
1      12     200     1.5
2      9      100     1.5
3      9      100     1.5
4      18     150     3
5      12     na      3
6      18     50      4.5
7      9      25      4.5
```
