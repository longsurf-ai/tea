Difference between `source` and its value `length` bars ago.

```tea
ta.change(source, int length = 1)
```

| Parameter | Type  | Default | Description                                                                     |
| --------- | ----- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `source`  |       |         | Series to compare.                                                              |
| `length`  | `int` | `1`     | Number of bars back, at least 1. Leaving it out compares with the previous bar. |

**Returns:** `float`, or `int` for an `int` source: the difference.

**Formula**

$$
\mathrm{change}_t = \mathit{source}_t - \mathit{source}_{t-\mathit{length}}
$$

**Warm-up and na:** `na` on the first `length` bars, and when either value is `na`.

**Example:** The change from the previous bar and from two bars back. The close is
missing at index 4, so `change` is `na` at indexes 4 and 5, and `change2` at
indexes 4 and 6.

```tea
emit "close" close
emit "change" ta.change(close)
emit "change2" ta.change(close, 2)
```

```csv
time,close
0,10
1,12
2,15
3,14
4,
5,18
6,20
```

**Output:**

```text
index  close  change  change2
0      10     na      na
1      12     2       na
2      15     3       5
3      14     -1      2
4      na     na      na
5      18     na      4
6      20     2       na
```

**Pine Script:** Pine also accepts a `bool` source, returning `true` when it differs
from the previous one; in Tea a `bool` source is a compile error.
