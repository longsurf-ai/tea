Directional Movement Index (DMI): the +DI and -DI lines and the Average Directional Index (ADX), each from `0` to `100`.

```tea
ta.dmi(simple int diLength, simple int adxSmoothing)
```

| Parameter      | Type         | Description                                                                                                                            |
| -------------- | ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `diLength`     | `simple int` | Smoothing length for +DI and -DI in bars, at least 1. It must be a value known when the script starts, such as a constant or an input. |
| `adxSmoothing` | `simple int` | Smoothing length for ADX in bars, at least 1. It must be a value known when the script starts, such as a constant or an input.         |

**Returns:** `[float, float, float]`: +DI, -DI and ADX.

On each bar, the rise of [`high`](../price-and-volume.md#high) is the upward move and the fall of
[`low`](../price-and-volume.md#low) the downward move; only the larger of the two is kept, and only
when it is positive. Each move and the true range ([`ta.tr`](./tr.md)) are
smoothed with [`ta.rma`](./rma.md) over `diLength` bars, and each smoothed move as
a percentage of the smoothed true range gives +DI and -DI. A bar counts
toward these three averages only when its high and low and the previous
bar's high, low and close are all known, so the first bar never counts.
ADX is `100` times the [`ta.rma`](./rma.md), over `adxSmoothing` bars, of the
absolute difference of +DI and -DI divided by their sum.

+DI and -DI leave `0` to `100` only when a close lies above its bar's high
or below its low.

**Formula**

$$
\begin{aligned}
u_t &= \mathit{high}_t - \mathit{high}_{t-1}, \qquad
d_t = \mathit{low}_{t-1} - \mathit{low}_t, \qquad
r_t = \mathrm{tr}(\text{false})_t \\
p_t &= \begin{cases} u_t & u_t > d_t \text{ and } u_t > 0 \\
0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases} \\
m_t &= \begin{cases} d_t & d_t > u_t \text{ and } d_t > 0 \\
0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases} \\
(\hat{p}_t, \hat{m}_t, \hat{r}_t) &= \begin{cases}
(p_t, m_t, r_t) & u_t, d_t, r_t \ne \text{na} \\
(\text{na}, \text{na}, \text{na}) & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases} \\
q^{+}_t &= \frac{100 \cdot \mathrm{rma}(\hat{p}, \mathit{diLength})_t}
{\mathrm{rma}(\hat{r}, \mathit{diLength})_t}, \qquad
q^{-}_t = \frac{100 \cdot \mathrm{rma}(\hat{m}, \mathit{diLength})_t}
{\mathrm{rma}(\hat{r}, \mathit{diLength})_t} \\
\mathrm{DI}^{\pm}_t &= \begin{cases} q^{\pm}_t & q^{\pm}_t \ne \text{na} \\
\mathrm{DI}^{\pm}_{t-1} & \text{otherwise} \end{cases} \\
x_t &= \frac{|\mathrm{DI}^{+}_t - \mathrm{DI}^{-}_t|}
{\mathrm{DI}^{+}_t + \mathrm{DI}^{-}_t},
\text{ or } 0 \text{ when both are } 0 \\
\mathrm{ADX}_t &= 100 \cdot \mathrm{rma}(x, \mathit{adxSmoothing})_t
\end{aligned}
$$

**Warm-up and na:** +DI and -DI are `na` until `diLength` bars have counted and a
counted bar has a true range other than `0`, so always on the first
`diLength` bars. After that, on a bar that does not count or where the
smoothed true range is `0`, they keep their values and ADX still averages
them. ADX is `na` until `adxSmoothing` values of +DI and -DI are known.

**Example:** +DI and -DI start at index 3, the third bar that counts: the smoothed moves,
`4 / 3` up and `2 / 3` down, as percentages of the smoothed true range,
`11 / 3`, are about 36.36 and 18.18. The missing close at index 5 keeps
index 6 from counting, so +DI and -DI keep their values there while ADX
still moves.

```tea
[plusDI, minusDI, adx] = ta.dmi(3, 2)
emit "high" high
emit "low" low
emit "close" close
emit "+DI" plusDI
emit "-DI" minusDI
emit "ADX" adx
```
