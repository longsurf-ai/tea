Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD) of `source`: the MACD line, its signal line and the histogram.

```tea
ta.macd(
    float source,
    simple int fastlen,
    simple int slowlen,
    simple int siglen
)
```

| Parameter | Type         | Description                                                                                                                               |
| --------- | ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `source`  | `float`      | Series to measure, usually [`close`](../price-and-volume.md#close).                                                                       |
| `fastlen` | `simple int` | Length of the fast average in bars, at least 1. It must be a value known when the script starts, such as a constant or an input.          |
| `slowlen` | `simple int` | Length of the slow average in bars, at least 1. It must be a value known when the script starts, such as a constant or an input.          |
| `siglen`  | `simple int` | Length of the signal line's average in bars, at least 1. It must be a value known when the script starts, such as a constant or an input. |

**Returns:** `[float, float, float]`: the MACD line, the signal line and the histogram.

The MACD line is the fast [`ta.ema`](./ema.md) of `source` minus the slow one,
the signal line is the [`ta.ema`](./ema.md) of the MACD line over the bars where
`source` is known, and the histogram is the MACD line minus the signal
line.

**Formula**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{macd}_t &= \mathrm{ema}(\mathit{source}, \mathit{fastlen})_t -
\mathrm{ema}(\mathit{source}, \mathit{slowlen})_t \\
\mathrm{signal}_t &= \mathrm{ema}(y, \mathit{siglen})_t, \qquad
y_t = \begin{cases} \mathrm{macd}_t & \mathit{source}_t \ne \text{na} \\
\text{na} & \text{otherwise} \end{cases} \\
\mathrm{histogram}_t &= \mathrm{macd}_t - \mathrm{signal}_t
\end{aligned}
$$

**Warm-up and na:** All three are `na` before the first known value of `source` and `0`
on it, because each average starts from its first value. On a bar where
`source` is `na`, all three keep their values.

**Example:** All three are 0 on the first bar, and at index 1 the fast average is 12 and
the slow one 11.5, so the MACD line is 0.5. The close is missing at index 4,
so all three keep their values there.

```tea
[macdLine, signalLine, histogram] = ta.macd(close, 2, 3, 2)
emit "close" close
emit "macd" macdLine
emit "signal" signalLine
emit "histogram" histogram
```
