Stochastic oscillator, the fast %K: where `source` sits between the lowest `l` and the highest `h` of the last `length` bars, from `0` to `100`.

```tea
ta.stoch(float source, float h, float l, int length)
```

| Parameter | Type    | Description                                                                                        |
| --------- | ------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `source`  | `float` | Series to place in the range, usually [`close`](../price-and-volume.md#close).                     |
| `h`       | `float` | Series whose highest value is the top of the range, usually [`high`](../price-and-volume.md#high). |
| `l`       | `float` | Series whose lowest value is the bottom of the range, usually [`low`](../price-and-volume.md#low). |
| `length`  | `int`   | Number of bars in the window, at least 1.                                                          |

**Returns:** `float`: the position as a percentage of the range.

It uses [`ta.lowest`](./lowest.md) and [`ta.highest`](./highest.md), and leaves `0` to `100`
only when `source` lies outside that range. The result is not smoothed:
pass it to [`ta.sma`](./sma.md) for the slow %K.

**Formula**

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{stoch}_t &= 100 \cdot \frac{\mathit{source}_t - L_t}{H_t - L_t} \\
\text{where}\quad H_t &= \mathrm{highest}(\mathit{h}, \mathit{length})_t \\
L_t &= \mathrm{lowest}(\mathit{l}, \mathit{length})_t
\end{aligned}
$$

**Warm-up and na:** `na` on the first `length - 1` bars, when `source` is `na`, when a
window has no known value, and when the range is `0`. Other `na` values are
left out of the windows.

**Example:** At index 2 the close of 10 is 3 above the lowest low, 7, in a range of 5,
giving 60. The missing low at index 3 is left out of the windows, and the
missing close at index 4 makes that bar `na`.

```tea
emit "high" high
emit "low" low
emit "close" close
emit "stoch" ta.stoch(close, high, low, 3)
```

```csv
time,high,low,close
0,10,8,9
1,12,9,11
2,11,7,10
3,13,,12
4,12,8,
5,15,10,14
6,14,9,10
7,16,11,15
```

**Output:**

```text
index  high  low  close  stoch
0      10    8    9      na
1      12    9    11     na
2      11    7    10     60
3      13    na   12     83.33333333333333
4      12    8    na     na
5      15    10   14     85.71428571428571
6      14    9    10     28.571428571428573
7      16    11   15     85.71428571428571
```

**Pine Script:** Pine names the second and third parameters `high` and `low`; Tea names
them `h` and `l`, so named arguments read `h = high, l = low`.
