Commodity Channel Index (CCI) of `source`.

```tea
ta.cci(float source, int length)
```

| Parameter | Type    | Description                                                       |
| --------- | ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `source`  | `float` | Series to measure, such as [`hlc3`](../price-and-volume.md#hlc3). |
| `length`  | `int`   | Number of bars in the window, at least 1.                         |

**Returns:** `float`: the index.

It is `source` minus its [`ta.sma`](./sma.md), divided by `0.015` times the mean
absolute deviation ([`ta.dev`](./dev.md)) of the same window. Values that are
`na` are left out of both.

**Formula**

$$
\mathrm{cci}_t = \frac{
\mathit{source}_t - \mathrm{sma}(\mathit{source}, \mathit{length})_t}
{0.015 \cdot \mathrm{dev}(\mathit{source}, \mathit{length})_t}
$$

**Warm-up and na:** `na` on the first `length - 1` bars, when `source` is `na`, and when
the known values in the window are all equal, as they always are for a
`length` of 1.

**Example:** At index 2 the close is 1 above the 3-bar mean of 11 and the mean deviation
is `2 / 3`, giving about 100. Index 5 has no close, and from index 7 the
known closes in the window are equal, so those bars are `na`.

```tea
emit "close" close
emit "cci" ta.cci(close, 3)
```

```csv
time,close
0,10
1,11
2,12
3,12
4,9
5,
6,12
7,12
8,12
```
