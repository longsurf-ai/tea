Parabolic SAR: a trailing stop that moves faster toward price as the trend makes new extremes.

```tea
ta.sar(start, inc, max)
```

| Parameter | Description                                              |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| `start`   | Initial acceleration factor, such as `0.02`.             |
| `inc`     | Increase of the acceleration factor at each new extreme. |
| `max`     | Largest acceleration factor.                             |

**Returns:** The level; `na` on the first bar, and on every bar if this call did not run on the bar where `bar_index` is `1`.

The trend starts on the bar where [`bar_index`](../bar-and-time.md#bar_index) is `1`: upward with the
level at the previous [`low`](../price-and-volume.md#low) if [`close`](../price-and-volume.md#close) rose, otherwise downward
with the level at the previous [`high`](../price-and-volume.md#high). Each bar the level moves toward
the trend's extreme price by the acceleration factor, which starts at `start`
and grows by `inc`, up to `max`, whenever the trend makes a new extreme. When
the bar's low falls below the level in an uptrend, or its high rises above it
in a downtrend, the trend reverses and the level jumps to the extreme of the
trend that ended. In an uptrend the level stays at or below the previous two
lows; in a downtrend, at or above the previous two highs.
